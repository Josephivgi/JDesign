# Minimalist

Few shapes, lots of empty space, muted colors. The example image was drawn in JDraw with these steps.

## The look

Minimalist art, very few simple shapes, large areas of negative space, muted earthy palette, calm balanced composition.

Avoid: Clutter, detail, busy textures, many objects.

## Palette

`#f2ebe0` background, `#e9e2d6` ground, `#d9663f` accent, `#2f3e46` dark, `#52796f` muted green

Add the colors you use with set_palette so the person can paint with them too.

## How to draw it in JDraw

- Five to ten items in the whole drawing. Each item earns its place.
- Large empty areas: at least half the canvas is background.
- Muted earthy palette with one warm accent (a single circle or shape).
- Simple geometry: a circle, a few triangles, one horizon line. Thin `line` items at opacity 0.3 to 0.4 hint at detail.
- Balance off-center: put the main shape on a third, not in the middle.
- Add a hint of paper grain (a few very faint specks) and make one shape slightly imperfect. Keep one light direction if you add shadow.
- Avoid (slop): symmetrical centered layouts, glow, a gradient on every shape, extra decoration. If an item has no purpose, delete it.

## Check

Call view_drawing when you are done and compare with the example: does it read as minimalist at a glance? Fix what does not.
