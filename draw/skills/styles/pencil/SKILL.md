# Pencil Sketch

Graphite on paper with soft shading and sketchy lines. The example image was drawn in JDraw with these steps.

## The look

Graphite pencil sketch, sketchy construction lines, soft hatched shading, smudged graphite on off-white sketchbook paper, monochrome.

Avoid: Color, ink, digital painting, clean vector.

## Palette

`#f4f1ea` sketchbook paper, `#3a3a3a` graphite, `#6b6b6b` soft graphite

Add the colors you use with set_palette so the person can paint with them too.

## How to draw it in JDraw

- Off-white sketchbook paper (#f4f1ea) as background, graphite gray (#3a3a3a) only.
- Outlines are 2 or 3 loose passes of the same line with a 2 to 3 px jitter, `pencil` brush, size about 2, opacity 0.7, pressure varying 0.4 to 0.8.
- Shading is soft hatching with the `pencil` brush: opacity 0.3 to 0.6, lighter pressure in light areas, a second crossing set for darks.
- Leave construction lines visible; do not clean up.
- A small handwritten note (Segoe Script) in soft gray suits a study page.
- Press harder on the shadow side and lighter on the lit side, and keep paper grain visible. Leave some areas unshaded.
- Avoid (slop): clean perfect outlines, even gray fill, copy-pasted hatching, a centered stiff subject. A little smudge and overshoot is right.

## Check

Call view_drawing when you are done and compare with the example: does it read as pencil sketch at a glance? Fix what does not.
